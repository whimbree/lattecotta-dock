# Lattecotta logo candidates, round three

Round three makes the parabolic zoom wave the hero: every candidate is
a row of things whose sizes rise to a peak and fall away again, the
shape a dock makes as the pointer sweeps it. Varlesh's three rising
dots hinted at this; the wave concept is kept, his geometry is not,
and per the round-three rules there is no script L anywhere. The style
carries over from round two: cream, warm peach, blush and fired
terracotta (it is in the name now), hand-thrown wobble instead of
perfect symmetry, rosy glow instead of masonry, no faces. The peak of
each wave is where the coffee identity lives: a filled latte or a
poured milk heart.

Shared palette: cream (#fdf0e0 to #fff9ef), milk (#fffdf6 to
#f7e3c6), warm peach (#ffd5a6, #eda96f), blush glow (#ff9d7a,
#ffa584), caramel crema (#e5b26f to #bd854a), fired terracotta
(#de855a to #b45c3c). Each candidate is one master SVG
(viewBox 0 0 48 48) with renders at 256, 48 and 16.
`contact-sheet.png` shows all four at 256, labeled. Numbering
continues from round two.

## Candidate 9: parabolic latte wave

Five top-down clay cups on a cream plate, bottoms on a common dock
line, sizes parabolic. The peak cup is the only full latte: caramel
crema and a poured milk heart, tilted with unequal lobes and the
pour's start dot. The wave reads instantly because the discs overlap
the way zoomed dock icons do, with cream keylines keeping the
neighbours separate and the peak drawn over them. Warmth comes from
the cream plate, a blush glow pooled behind the peak, and the fired
clay rims. At 16px it holds the best of the four with a background:
a warm tile, a dark rising-and-falling mound, and a light heart spot
still visible at the peak.

## Candidate 10: clay tile zoom

The most literal one: five kiln-fired ceramic tiles, the dock's icon
row itself, floating free with no plate. Sizes rise to a peak and
fall, each tile tilted a little as if nudged mid-sweep. The peak tile
is the terracotta one, carrying the milk heart and a curl of steam
that fades as it climbs; the side tiles stay plain peach ceramic, so
the one zoomed app is unmistakably the latte. Warmth: the rosy glow
behind the peak and the peach-to-caramel tile gradient. At 16px the
side tiles thin to warm dashes and the icon becomes a terracotta
square with a light heart over a soft row; legible, but the softest
16 of the round.

## Candidate 11: the espresso line

Side view: five cream cups on a caramel bar, sizes swelling to a
peak and easing off, the zoom made of crockery. The peak cup is full,
steaming, with a droopy low-set handle that reaches over the
neighbour it magnifies past; an ember pools on the bar beneath it,
fresh from the kiln. Every cup tilts a degree or three and thin
ceramic edges keep cream on cream separated. The wave reads in the
cup heights against the flat bar. Warmth: the caramel bar, the blush
halo, the ember under the peak. At 16px the cups reduce to cream
lumps on a warm bar with the peak still tallest; the steam and handle
go first.

## Candidate 12: the wave, poured

The quiet one: a single big top-down latte in a hand-thrown cream
cup, and the wave is drawn by the milk itself as string-of-pearls
latte art: pearls swelling to a poured heart at the peak, dragged
through by one thin pour line, the way a barista actually chains
hearts. The dock lives inside the coffee. Warmth: kiln glow rising
through the lower rim under the milk, wobbled rims, milk on deep
crema. At 16px it degrades to a warm disc with a bright wave band
and a heart hint at the center, the strongest small-size identity of
the floaters and still clearly a latte at a glance.
