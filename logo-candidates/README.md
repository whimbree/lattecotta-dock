# Lattecotta logo candidates

Four directions for the Lattecotta icon, replacing the rejected first
attempt (b5d91c0d). That one failed for concrete reasons: three
concentric rings of similar weight read as a target instead of a cup,
the rim gradient went muddy rust at the bottom, the heart had a drip
tail, and nothing of the original identity (the script L, the three
dock dots) survived. At 48px it collapsed to an orange donut.

Each candidate is one master SVG (viewBox 0 0 48 48) with renders at
256, 48 and 16. `contact-sheet.png` shows all four side by side.
Shared palette: terracotta clay (#d2693e to #a84b28), ember
(#ffab4f family), the original caramel crema (#e8b971 to #bd8d48)
and milk cream (#fff7eb to #f3e0c1).

## Candidate 1: kiln-rimmed original

Varlesh's mark untouched: the script L, the three dock dots and the
Breeze diagonal shadow on the caramel crema disc, now rimmed with a
fired terracotta ring so the cup itself is the kiln-fired ceramic.
It works because it keeps every recognizable element of the existing
identity and adds exactly one new idea. At 16px it degrades the same
way the original does: a warm disc with a hint of the mark, now with
a clay edge that still separates it from plain circles.

## Candidate 2: kiln arch

A squat brick kiln with an ember-lit arch and a latte cup silhouetted
inside, mid-firing; three vent holes across the dome echo the dock
indicator dots. The bright-on-dark opening gives it the strongest
value contrast of the four and the tombstone silhouette is unlike any
stock Breeze icon. At 16px the cup melts into the glow but the arch
plus ember gradient still reads immediately as a lit kiln.

## Candidate 3: milk heart on clay

Top-down latte in a fired terracotta cup: two zones only (clay rim,
caramel crema), a thin ember seam where they meet, and a poured milk
heart with its start dot as the hero mark. It is the closest to the
classic "latte app" reading while the ember seam and clay rim carry
the kiln story quietly. At 16px the heart simplifies to a soft cream
blob on caramel inside a clay ring, still clearly a latte.

## Candidate 4: cup as kiln, docked

The brave one: a terracotta cup with fire rising from inside (the cup
is the kiln), floating above a charcoal dock bar with three ember
indicator dots, Latte's floating-dock geometry drawn literally. It
says dock, coffee and fire in one silhouette and is the only candidate
that names the product category. At 16px it thins to flame, cup and a
dark bar with warm dots; less detail survives than in 1 to 3 but the
stack stays legible.
