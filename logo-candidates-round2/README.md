# Lattecotta logo candidates, round two

Round one (logo-candidates/, candidates 1 to 4) was competent but cold:
hard geometry, perfect circles, mirror symmetry, and a literal reading
of "kiln-fired" as burnt orange and brick brown. The verdict on it was
that cuteness lives in soft asymmetry and that warmth on screen is
cream, peach and blush with a rosy glow, not masonry. Round two draws
only from that: every shape here has a wobble or a tilt, the kiln
survives as a soft ember glow at a base or under a cup, and steam and
poured milk carry the life. No faces; these still have to sit in a
serious panel.

Shared palette: cream (#fdf0e0 to #fff9ef), warm peach (#f6c390,
#ffd0a0), blush rose glow (#ff9d7a, #ffa584), caramel (#cf9455 to
#a9713a). Each candidate is one master SVG (viewBox 0 0 48 48) with
renders at 256, 48 and 16. `contact-sheet.png` shows all four at 256,
labeled. Numbering continues from round one.

## Candidate 5: poured heart, hand-thrown bowl

Top-down latte in a cream ceramic bowl. Both rims are drawn with a
wheel-thrown wobble (no perfect circles anywhere), the crema is a deep
caramel-peach, and the hero mark is a properly poured milk heart:
tilted six degrees, lobes slightly unequal, with the drag-tail droop
where the pour pulled through, plus the little start dot above.
The warmth comes from the cream-on-caramel pairing and a rosy ember
glow rising through the bowl's lower rim, painted under the milk so
the heart stays crisp. At 16px it simplifies to a cream heart on a
warm disc inside a pale ring, still instantly a latte.

## Candidate 6: cozy cup, docked

Side view of a squat cream cup, tilted three degrees, with a droopy
round handle sitting a bit low, three asymmetric steam wisps of
different heights and sway, and a peach coffee surface with a milk
gleam. The cup sits on a warm caramel saucer-bar carrying the three
dock dots in cream, so the floating-dock identity survives without a
charcoal slab. Warmth: a rosy halo pools behind and under the cup, and
the cup body blushes toward its base as if just out of the kiln. At
16px the steam drops out but cup, handle bump and warm bar with dots
all hold.

## Candidate 7: script L, kiln-warmed

Varlesh's hand-drawn script L, verbatim, in cream on a hand-poured
peach blob whose corners each round off differently; the L is tilted
four degrees and the three dock dots are redrawn with a wobble in size
and height. The blob glows rosier toward its base, which is all that
remains of the kiln. This is the continuity candidate: everything
recognizable from the original icon, re-fired in the warm palette.
At 16px it degrades best of the four, a warm rounded square with a
bright curl and three dots, unmistakable in a panel next to Breeze
icons.

## Candidate 8: steam heart

The quiet one: a small squat cup, seen slightly from above, whose
steam gathers into one soft translucent heart, tilted eight degrees
with unequal lobes, still connected to the coffee by a thin curling
wisp, with a second stray wisp escaping to the side. The warmth is in
the idea itself (the cup loves you back) plus the rosy ember glow
under the base. It is the most delicate of the four: at 48px it is
fully alive; at 16px it reduces to a pale heart floating over a warm
cup, readable but soft-spoken, and would want a bolder size variant
if it wins.
